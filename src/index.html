How it works

Type the clinical impression the way you would say it: “chest pain”, “AFib RVR”, “kidney stone”, “peds fever”, an abbreviation (SOB, AMS, GIB) or an ICD-10 code. You get every matching template: MDMs, discharge instructions, decision rules and procedure notes.

Tap a card to copy its blank template. Or open Fill in and copy: tap the options you want, type into the blanks, then copy the finished text. “Full note” cards build the whole note for one impression; scores have a Calculate panel.

*** marks a blank (in Epic, F2 jumps to the next one). {this / that} marks a choice. Anything you leave unfilled copies that way, so you can finish it in the chart.

The dot names (.cpmdm, .afibmdm) are this app’s own. If your group already has a phrase for the same thing, use theirs.

My phrases

Open any card and tap Make my version to edit it, or write a new one. Your phrases are saved on this device only and show up in search with everything else.

A group pack is a phrase file shared by your group, such as your department’s SmartPhrases. Imported phrases keep the group’s name as a tag. Export first if you’re moving to a new phone.

Shared library

When you save a phrase you can also send it to the shared library. Each one is reviewed before anyone else sees it. Approved phrases appear under “Community”.

Before you sign

These are original templates written for this app. They are not the official SmartPhrases of any hospital, health system or physician group. They are documentation aids, not clinical guidelines: confirm drug doses, decision rules and local protocols, and delete anything you didn’t do. Each card shows whether it has had a named clinical review.

Spanish discharge instructions should be checked by a certified medical interpreter before routine use.

Diagnosis codes are typical ICD-10-CM examples from the CDC code files, not a complete list. The right code comes from the documentation.

What you type into blanks is never saved and never leaves this device. Stars, recent phrases and your own phrases stay on this device. The only things ever sent are phrases you choose to share, and (if you turn it on) empty search words. Don’t enter patient identifiers anywhere.
